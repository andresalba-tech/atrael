## Selected AI Projects

### Atrael

Atrael is a private, local-first AI workstation designed and developed by Andres Alba.

It combines:

* Local LLM inference
* Multiple AI models
* Multimodal image understanding
* PDF and DOCX analysis
* Spreadsheet analysis
* Large-document processing
* Exhaustive chunk analysis
* Hierarchical consolidation
* Streaming responses
* AI performance observability
* Local text-to-speech

Atrael was designed to explore how capable an AI workstation can become while running primarily on consumer hardware and without depending on commercial cloud AI APIs.
